Combined scopes

Few brands need only one of these. A website without upkeep goes stale; a brand without content stays quiet. Booked together, they're quoted as one project — usually 10–15% below separate scopes, since the strategy only gets done once.

How it works

  • 50% to begin, 50% on delivery. Retainers are billed monthly in advance.
  • Retainers run three months minimum, then roll on with 30 days notice either side.
  • Two rounds of revisions on project work; further rounds are quoted separately.
  • Timelines begin once the brief, assets and access have been received.
  • Ad spend, domains, hosting, stock and paid plugins are billed at cost or paid by you directly.
  • Anything outside an agreed scope is quoted before it starts — never added to the invoice after.
  • Development work beyond the time a retainer includes is billed at ₹1,200 an hour.
